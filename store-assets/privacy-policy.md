## Privacy Policy · 隐私政策 · 隱私權政策

The privacy policy is published as one file per language:

- [English](privacy-policy.en.md) — HTML: [`privacy-policy.en.html`](privacy-policy.en.html)
- [简体中文](privacy-policy.zh-CN.md) — HTML: [`privacy-policy.zh-CN.html`](privacy-policy.zh-CN.html)
- [繁體中文](privacy-policy.zh-TW.md) — HTML: [`privacy-policy.zh-TW.html`](privacy-policy.zh-TW.html)

`privacy-policy.html` is a language picker that opens the version matching the visitor's browser language.
